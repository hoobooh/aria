Forius, formerly known as *Fortus* before over half of its land mass collapsed during the Celeste Destia, is an abnormally warm and mineral-rich continent comprised almost entirely of stone and metal. It is the second smallest continent in Flaes Asteliar after the Rue/Rakir cluster. It sits south of Primera and is usually draw on the bottom of world maps. It is a sparsely populated continent, housing only 2~3 million people concentrated on its coastlines. The continent is home to the Forius Federation, a coalition of small city states who cooperate for mutual economic growth.

Forius has a hot and dry climate, experiencing extreme weather and having almost no vegetation. Forius is surrounded by Ancient ruins and relics buried underwater. The center of the continent leaks so much heat that staying above ground away from the coastlines is considered suicide. Forius's landscape is composed of large steep mountains that climb past the clouds, but are more well known for the vast mineral deposits locked underneath. Forius supplies almost the entire world's metal supply, remaining a popular destination for industrial traders and those looking to join the never ending demand for more. Forius is also notable for lava rivers, which occasionally bubble to the surface and flow down into the water.

The coasts of the continent are not free of danger; old Enka creations from during the Celeste Destia occasionally grow active and prowl the waters below, making Ancient excavation and overseas travel dangerous. Unique magical beasts born in the mana-infused lava rarely manifest and attack cities or miners. Undiscovered tunnels connect Forius's underground caverns to the Endless Abyss, causing unwelcome surprises as Abyssals find their way through into the continent from below. Perhaps most dangerous are the flash winters, in which Forius will suddenly undergo a drastic cold spell and plummet to freezing temperatures for weeks at a time. Many choose to reside underground in old Ancient strongholds that insulate heat well or stick close to the coastlines, where heating infrastructure is well developed.

Forius is also well known to explorers and scholars for its buried Ancient strongholds, built by Remnants and Enkas underground long ago. While some are damaged beyond repair, many were in good enough condition that new settlers colonized them. Strongholds built for war, however, house their own collection of dangers— barring the first cities that have been inhabited for a while, exploring new underground strongholds remains an overly expensive and risky process due to potential structural collapses and Ancient war machines that remain active. Most discovered strongholds are closed off by their respective city states, but ambitious explorers and scavengers are always found sneaking in at their own expense.
