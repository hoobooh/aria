Judicia, formerly known as *Justicia* before the Celeste Destia, is the biggest known megalith in Flaes Asteliar. By technicality, it is actually seven different megaliths clustered together and anchored to the main megalith through Ancient Starborn technology that no one can control anymore. Judicia sits directly north of Galeo and east of the Ruien continent. It is a mildly populated megalith, housing half a million Starborn on its three main megaliths. The megalith cluster contains only Judicia, the Starborn theocracy that the cluster is named after.

Judicia's weather is entirely dependent on the Endless Abyss below that it rests on. Abyss Storms are a common occurrence, though the megalith cluster rests high enough above that the damage isn't destructive. The megaliths experience minor floods from rising mana condensing into water below, resulting in replenishing wells of ground water and nutrient-rich soil. The megaliths hover lower to the Abyss in winter, likely caused by accrued frozen mass not dispersing as it's supposed to. Judicia experiences a mix of rain and snow in the winter. Powdered mana crystals frequently snow from the sky, allowing the megaliths to sustain their magic consumption without excessive exploration or danger. The air is thin and crisp, smelling slightly of rain. Golden grain sprouts in the spring with the same denseness as grass, illuminating the whole cluster with an ethereal glow that visitors described as another world.

Judicia suffers very little in terms of danger. Despite being directly above the Endless Abyss, their altitude makes it virtually impossible for the vast majority of Abyssals to even attempt to climb up. Rare flying Abyssals do occasionally rise within view, but Judician defense systems make it difficult for them to do more than cause the occasional scare. As most Judicians avoid living near the megaliths' edges, it is difficult for stray Abyssals to cause excessive damage or reach living people. Magical beasts rarely form on the cluster due to the Judician theocracy's meticulous maintenance of the island. When they do, they are usually swiftly exterminated by the Judician Inquisition. The sheer distance from Judicia to any other major landmass makes national defense extremely simply; it is simply impossible for any large scale invasions to happen, either by man or beast.

Of the ruins left behind by Ancients, Judicia's are likely the most well preserved. So well preserved, in fact, that some of their megastructures remain active. While many parts of Judicia broke away when the world collapsed, the megastructures hidden within Justicia's architecture seems to have held firm. The size of the main megalith, too, seems only possible due to an artificial core embedded somewhere within the main megalith. The leading theory regarding the original purpose of this function points to Judicia's original geography, which is marked on maps as an island nation. It is likely that Justicia was never an actual island— merely the first megalith, built to float in the water and inadvertently avoiding the same fate as the continents around them. The mystery of their design remains unsolved— attempts to study any part of the Judician megastructures are strictly forbidden out of fear that tampering may interrupt their functionalities.
