Primera, derived from the term *Prima*, is the largest continent in Flaes Asteliar. It sits north of Forius and has the most contact with the Endless Abyss. It is by far the most populous continent, housing roughly 10 to 20 million people spread across the entire continent. The continent includes the two empires Arkadia and Ardentia, the former being in the North and the latter controlling Central and Southern Primera.

Primera is a fairly temperate continent, experiencing warm weather and sporting large forests. Northern Primera is covered in arid mountains and scattered forests, experiencing mild rain and snow during the winter. Southern Primera is almost tropical in nature, having large trees and dense rainforests covering the entire region. Central Primera, however, suffers from extreme weather behavior caused by the Heart's Abyss. The outskirts of the Heart's Abyss snow with crystallized mana flakes, piling on the ground and inciting magical beasts to gather and form stampedes. Past the outskirts, violent storms and thundering clouds circle the center with virtually no pause. Above the actual Heart's Abyss, however, weather is almost serene. Those who make it past the mana flake fields will find a clear quiet sky and a crisp fog blanketing the entire Abyss. The peaceful surface only serves to hide the dangers underneath, however— giant Abyssals and ancient constructs lurk inside the Abyss, waiting for any unwitting creatures to fly within their midst.

To the north of the Heart's Abyss, a harsh mountain range prevents any rogue Abyssals from moving further north. As a result, almost all Abyssals end up traveling south to Ardentian territories. The sheer volume of Abyssals swarming from the Heart's Abyss necessitates constant vigilance and combat by Ardentia, which stands between Primera being lost to the Abyssals and the rest of the world. The mountain range protecting Ardentia from Abyssals, however, is the perfect birthing ground for magical beasts. The thick layers of mana flakes actively create hordes of magical beasts and form valuable mana crystals that attract prospectors from all over the world. Arkadia cannot access over half of its territory safely due to the constant magical beast threat pouring down from the mountains. Likely due to the Abyssals moving south, magical beasts usually avoid the infested southern routes and head north.

Primera also has the most islands out of any continent in Asteliar. A single large atoll in the west, called Fleuria, hosts a plethora of harbor cities responsible for facilitating trade and production. The western end of the atoll contains a significant portion of Arkadia's navy, staying on guard for the neighboring Endless Abyss and potential foreign threats. An affluent Arkadian city called Excella also resides on a large isolated island to the west, overlooking Ardentian territory just a few days' travel away. A massive cluster of islands lies east of the Heart's Abyss, uninhabited by all due to the violent Abyss Storms that wreak havoc on them. Southern Primera's islands, dubbed the Trident's Bay, plays a similar role to Fleuria. Ardentia's navy is mostly stationed and maintained there, using the refuge of the islands as cover from southern Abyss Storms.
