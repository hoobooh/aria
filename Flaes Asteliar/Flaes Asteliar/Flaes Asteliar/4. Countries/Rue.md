Rue, derived from the Ruien race that primarily inhabits the country, is a nation located on the northern half of Reis.

Rue occupies the entire northern island that makes up Reis's north. Rue has been in an active power struggle with its neighboring country, Rakir, since the two originally split apart long ago. Rue is ruled by the descendants of their founder's seventh son, aptly named Rue. They maintain a hereditary dynasty, though they occasionally adopt newcomers into their bloodline.

Rue is somewhat unsophisticated, hampered by economic stifling and a dysfunctional government. Rue's literacy rate is somewhat low. Most of the population is comprised of farmers and laborers, undergoing little to no formal education. They have little technology aside from the main capital in the Ruien Valley, which is built atop the ruins of the former Ancient city that once stood there. Rue is not a popular destination for immigration, though they are noticeably lax about allowing newcomers in.

## Diplomacy

Rue does not maintain many diplomatic relations with countries other than Rakir, but they are on good terms with Judicia. Most countries adjacent to Primera opt to connect with their political rival Rakir due to its substantially safer trade routes. Rue, as the only country to warmly welcome Judician influence, receives financial aid from their eastern neighbor. Rue is politically tense with Rakir. They have maintained an uneasy peace for at least 150 years, but have a long history of violent feuds between each other.

## Trade

Rue is an unpopular trading hub due to its unfortunate location. Aspiring traders either have to circle through the Old Arkadian sea or pass through Rakir. In the case of the former, the Old Arkadian sea remains a place of dangerously freezing temperatures with unpredictable Abyss storm behavior. In the case of the latter, it is simply more beneficial to directly deal from within Rakir. The only exceptions to the rule are Judicia and the Machiya Merchant Fleet, both of which are in geographically convenient positions to deal with Rue.

Rue's main exports are sweet beads and Ruien beef. Sweet beads, also called softgrain in some cultures, is a sweet circular grain that dissolves easily in water. While not exclusively native to Rue, the country's deep-rooted sweet bead culture keeps them the grain's main producer. Rakir sells a different variant of sweet beads at lower quantities, though most food contemporaries seem to agree that Rue's harvests taste better. Ruien beef suffers from the inverse of this reputation, often being described as tough and difficult to complement with seasoning.
