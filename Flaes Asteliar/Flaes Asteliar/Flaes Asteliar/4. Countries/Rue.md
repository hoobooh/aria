Rue, derived from the Ruien race that primarily inhabits the country, is a nation located on the northern half of the Ruien continent.

Rue occupies the entire northern island that makes up the Ruien continent's north. Rue has been in an active power struggle with its neighboring country, Rakir, since the two originally split apart long ago. Rue is ruled by the descendants of their founder's seventh son, aptly named Rue. They maintain a hereditary dynasty, though they occasionally adopt newcomers into their bloodline.

Rue is somewhat unsophisticated, hampered by economic stifling and a dysfunctional government. Rue's literacy rate is somewhat low. Most of the population is comprised of farmers and laborers, undergoing little to no formal education. They have little technology aside from the main capital in the Ruien Valley, which is built atop the ruins of the former Ancient city that once stood there. Rue is politically tense with Rakir, having undergone large-scale wars in the distant past. They have maintained an uneasy peace for at least 150 years. Rue is not a popular destination for immigration, though they are noticeably lax about allowing newcomers in.

## Diplomacy

Rue does not maintain many diplomatic relations with countries other than Rakir, but they are on good terms with Judicia. As most countries adjacent to Primera opt to connect with their political rival Rakir due to its substantially safer trade routes. Rue, as the only country to warmly welcome Judician influence, receives financial aid from their eastern neighbor.
