
The Machiya Merchant Fleet is a prolific merchant coalition that conducts trade on a massive scale utilizing unique air technology.

The Machiya Merchant Fleet, commonly called Machiya, is a relatively new coalition that belongs to no country. The Machiya Merchant Fleet is a trio of three supermassive airships that each host populations the size of cities aboard them. Powered by ancient Titan crystals once thought to not exist anymore, it is believed that the fleet could stay afloat for as long as the ships remain structural intact.

Machiya is extremely sophisticated. They boast the second highest literacy rate in the world. They maintain artificial environments aboard their supermassive airships, cited to host small forests and rivers inside along with compact farms and crystal dust refiners to manufacture their own food and energy. There is essentially no conflict aboard the ships. Machiya enforces its own set of laws on fleet through artificer-controlled golems and several militias that live aboard. Machiya is a popular immigration destination due to its high quality of living and security, but limited space and tight competition makes it difficult to actually join the Fleet.

## Diplomacy

Machiya maintains strong diplomatic relations with almost every country, their trade and influx of wealth being viewed as an economic boon to whichever country they deign to visit. Judicia alone bars Machiya from entering its skies, citing security issues and the risk that a potential collision could cause to the megaliths. While Machiya does not take part in political conflicts, they are known to occasionally rescue stranded ships in need of peril. In preparation for this possibility, some sailors and travelers purchase pricy Machiya Flares from the fleet, which loudly announce their presence to the skies above so that the passing Machiya fleet can spot and rescue them. Though similar flares not sold by Machiya exist, many prefer the peace of mind using Machiya's own rather than a flare that may be considered unfamiliar. While on friendly terms, most governments observe the fleet with strong caution. With most major military operations drastically limited by the lack of large scale long-distance aerial travel, Machiya presents both a unique opportunity and a threat. Machiya has gone on record to state that it has no plans to participate in any armed conflicts between nations. With so many different merchants aboard, many view Machiya as a safe neutral zone for diplomatic discussions and private meetings— a role that Machiya accepts graciously provided that appropriate payment is given.

## Trade

Machiya deals in essentially every possible resource and commodity. The Fleet sometimes doesn't even sell items themselves, instead allowing merchants aboard to buy and sell goods at their own leisure and safety in exchange for a hefty tax. Many merchant guilds sign lengthy contracts with Machiya to secure better terms for themselves in exchange for exclusivity, essentially becoming contracted Machiya merchants that do the heavy legwork off the fleet. While the Fleet hosts a diverse market, perhaps even more famous is its relatively cheap prices compared to other foreign importers. Trips aboard the Machiya Merchant Fleet do not incur extra expenses from crystal consumption or security, and as such can be sold at much lower prices than its competitors. Merchants not associated with Machiya are indirectly pressured to join in one form or another, as they could theoretically earn more to a wider margin of customers. The only weakness of the Fleet's mercantile capabilities is its travel time, which prevents it from visiting the same region more than once every two years.

## Military

Machiya maintains a strong military force despite its supposed non-involvement policy. It hosts small armies of refined golems aboard its ships and regularly employs mercenaries to maintain security when needed. Those who choose to live aboard the Fleet permanently have the option of joining Machiya's actual garrisons, trained by retired mercenaries and veterans from other countries. Machiya's militias are functionally city guards who patrol the fleet's homes and pathways while occasionally being called to participate in rescue operations for stranded groups off-fleet. Their militias do not have a strong weapons code and expect their soldiers to manage their own equipment. Advanced runed equipment such as muskets or other firearms are uncommon but standard martial weapons are commonly used aboard the fleet.

## Organizations

Machiya hosts several notable organizations to sustain its operations including the Machiya Arcanum. The Machiya Arcanum is the artificers' guild started by Machiya's original founder, commonly credited with having designed Machiya's three supermassive ships. It jealously guards its secrets and rarely recruits new members from the public. While most of the fleet is fairly open about newcomers and passage, the Machiya Arcanum maintains a strict travel code that prevents its members from leaving or traveling without prior notice. The Machiya Arcanum is responsible for sensitive ship repairs and maintenance near the Titans' cores, putting them within the circle of the fleet's most trusted. The Arcanum also creates and maintains the fleet's security golems.

Machiya's militias follow the jurisdiction of the Featherfoot Knight Order, which was founded in Ardentia but moved aboard Machiya soon after the fleet first launched. The Featherfoot Knight Order dons caps fitted with two long feathers from gold-beak fogbirds, an avian species known for persisting in Abyss fog rather than above land. They don colors of deep green and usually wield muskets and rapiers. While the Featherfoot Knights are competent fighters, they prioritize discipline and prudence more than they do pure martial power. Though once comprised of Ardentian veterans, the Featherfoot Knights now welcome any Machiya residents that hold no conflicting interests. Their main responsibilities are to train Machiya's militias and lead them when moving off-fleet. 

Machiya maintains a strong intelligence network that is managed by the Machiya Information Guild. The Information Guild, though not the only one of its kind, boasts a strong database of information from the frequent visits from merchants all around the world. Rather than keep its information private, most of the Machiya Information Guild's information is shared freely amongst residents. The Guild's information usually pertains to political rumors, trade updates, and potential dangers around the world. Sensitive or dangerous information, if received, is generally pulled from the Guild entirely and locked away for only Machiya's inner circle to know. The Machiya Information Guild also manages the Machiya Couriers' Guild, which facilitates messages and deliveries in and out of Machiya.

## History

The Machiya Merchant Fleet was first established by an Enka named Shisheshi Machiya roughly two centuries ago. Shisheshi Machiya was originally a scholar researching the Abyss that reportedly discovered active Titan crystals after he went missing on an expedition to a small megalith east of Arkadia. It is unclear where those cores were found; the megalith showed no signs of such a strong energy signature and Shisheshi was documented to be adamantly silent regarding the details. Shisheshi remained missing for 7 years before emerging with the rough prototypes of the Machiya Merchant Fleet's supermassive airships. While originally without much functionality other than flight. Shisheshi and his Machiya Arcanum quickly revolutionized the fleet's capabilities, their wealth continuing to grow as they traveled the world without restriction. Shisheshi eventually stepped down peacefully, stating that he had lived to his utmost satisfaction. He would pass away a few decades later in a large estate in Arkadia's Excella, which had recently begun building a massive city around an ancient Colossal machine.

Machiya only emerged as an economic superpower after Shisheshi's heir, Shishesha, took over. Shishesha aggressively expanded Machiya's operations and started hosting foreign merchants aboard. After negotiating new deals with relevant governments to exempt themselves from trade taxes and overhauling the conditions inside the originally barebones airships, Machiya began to experience rapid growth. Particularly notable were Shisheshi's ambitious carrier system that hosted smaller airships inside their main fleet, treating their motherships as carriers. While occasionally seen aboard naval ships, Machiya's ability to do so while keeping their carriers in the air elevated goods circulation and began spreading their economic influence even further. Shishesha retired recently, entrusting the Machiya Merchant Fleet to a young adopted Enka known as Shiyo. While breaking the existing bloodline generated some controversy, Shishesha's strong leadership has left little doubt regarding the new heir's competence.
