
The Galeo Islands are a loose term used to refer to the collection of warring island nations north of Galeo. 

Due to how unstable the governments are in this region, names are usually not assigned on any maps or texts. In the past decade alone, 7 of the islands have overthrown their leaders and instated new governments with new names. The Galeo Islands are the only known place in Flaes Asteliar where conflicts intensify to the point of civil war— likely due to the lack of livable space and resources in Galeo.

The Galeo Islands are surprisingly sophisticated despite their instability. They boast higher literacy rates than Arkadia and approach political matters with delicacy. The Islands, however, suffer from a constant depopulation of qualified people immigrating to stable countries such as Arkadia or Ardentia. Unable to further develop their countries without specialized workers, conflicts between similarly desperate islands over resources and land only continue to intensify.

## Diplomacy

Many within the Islands hold a deep-harbored resentment for their southern Regalreian neighbors. Stemming back to the founding of their respective countries, Regalreia's adamant refusal to even acknowledge the plight of the Islands has been a common criticism of the neglected Islanders. Regalreian natives are shunned and driven out. Products from the empire are frequently discarded out of principle. Traders from Arkadia and Ardentia, on the other hand, are frequent visitors and warmly welcomed by the Islands. Those from Primera are usually given mutual protection by troubled islands, regardless of the conflicts that might be happening at the time. The Machiya Merchant Fleet in particular is greeted by large festivals and joyous occasions when they arrive, thought of positively for the large influx of cheap supplies in exchange for unrefined resources harvested in the Islands and the nearby deserts. Many Islanders opt to depart aboard the Fleet permanently, seeking new homes in Primera or simply choosing the live with the Fleet itself.

## Trade

The Islands are known primarily for two trades: crystal dust collection and Elderwood, a type of wood harvested exclusively from local trees that ages extremely quickly. Crystal dust collection is done primarily through collecting mana flakes in the spring and fall, along with riskier operations to harvest the dust directly from the Curse-rampant Blood Desert in the south. Islanders harvest Elderwood from Elderwood trees, which grow in a very specific range from the main craters that host the Northern Galeo Bay and Southern Galeo Bay. Trees too far from the bays grow too stunted, while trees too close fail to grow at all. The northern areas of the Galeo Islands play a valuable role in sustaining their lives, the Elderwood forests supplying them with their iconic Elderwood— extremely tough and durable wooden material found only in the Islands. Despite being a large cluster of tropical islands, the Islands rarely rely on fishing or water-related harvests. The seas near the Galeo bays are complete lifeless, resulting in small fish populations that cannot be harvested in substantial quantities.

## Military

The Islands are well-known for producing sturdy warriors with large cutting weapons, possessing the second highest Astella birthrate compared to other nations. Ranged weaponry such as siege equipment and firearms are rarely used as the clustered trees make it extremely difficult to use them effectively. Their navies, on the other hand, are possibly the most advanced ones in Flaes Asteliar. Lined with self-loading cannons and boasting impressive speeds for their size, it is no exaggeration to say that over half of the Islands' wealth is spent developing and maintaining it. Their navies are perhaps the most busy, too— their fleets undergo intense combat from Abyssals, magical beasts, and neighboring fleets infringing on their territory.

## Organizations

While prone to inter-island conflict, the Islands maintain a council of representatives known as the Galeo Tribunal that aims to reduce the death toll and limit combat extremity. The Tribunal has no independent army, instead partly comprised of neutral representatives who reside on unclaimed islands. Most of the Tribunal is comprised of the islands' leaders themselves, though the members change frequently due to political upheaval. Usually, the Tribunal's main role is not to intervene in conflicts but to pause any ongoing troubles in order to deal with foreign threats such as Abyssal hordes and major magical beasts threatening the Islands as a whole.

A trading group known as the Elderwood Merchant Group regulates tree harvesting, ensuring that the Islanders do not accidentally starve themselves by overharvesting their resources. They maintain a small private navy that hunts foreign smugglers and guards the outer borders of the Islands to ensure that their prized trees remain within the control of the Islands. The Elderwood Merchant Group holds a unique political power within the Islands, able to imprison and judge any Islanders who they deem threatens the security of their forests. While complaints and criticisms can arise from the public, the group's wealth and influence are usually enough to stifle any major issues. The Elderwood Merchant Group is responsible for buying and selling harvested Elderwood, acting as a prolific middleman that buys from Islanders and stores the goods in safety. They sell directly to foreign traders as well as the Machiya Merchant Fleet, both of which buy wood in bulk quantities that individual harvesters wouldn't be able to satisfy. The Elderwood Merchant Group is politically tense with Tribunal due to their overwhelming economic influence.

Mercenaries group occasionally show, though the long travel makes them a rare sight. A particular group called the Long Horns has stayed for decades, offering their services in inter-island conflicts and beast suppressions. They don large horned helmets and long polearms meant for open combat, sticking mostly to the waters and beaches of the Islands. The Long Horns were once considered a suspicious group due to their lack of loyalty to any cause, but have slowly grown to be a reliable neutral arbiter of conflicts due to their deep understanding of Island politics and strict adherence to Tribunal rulings. Some in the Islands remain cautious as the Long Horns continue to grow in popularity and size. It is believed that the group may soon grow large enough to occupy an island entirely— a concern not unknown to the Tribunal which continues to keep a close eye on them.

## History

The Galeo Islands' history is perhaps the bloodiest of them all. The origin of both the Islands and Regalreia traces back to Shafur the Sage, who led the survivors of the Celeste Destia in the region to safety near the bays. According to well known Regalreian legend, the Sage insisted that group move south towards where Regalreia is now founded. The majority of the Sage's people were reluctant to leave the well-developed homes of their cities in what would be called the Blood Desert. The Sage eventually left with his believers, moving south and founding Regalreia. Those who chose to stay, however, quickly discovered Galeo's Curse and migrated north to the Galeo Islands. Islander legend, on the other hand, paints the situation in a different manner. It claims that the Sage had not actually warned the people, instead opting to take a small portion of the people he could control before the Curse had fully manifested. The ones left behind, when they tried to move south, were driven out by the Sage's newly founded nation and forced to live on the northern Islands. While the details of the Sage's involvement have conflicting records, it is generally agreed that a power struggle split the original Ancient civilization and caused each fracture to go where each nation is located today. Scholars estimate that most of the settlers who originally moved north succumbed to the Curse before they had fully traversed the continent.

Power struggles continued without noticeable progress until the influx of the first Abyssal hordes, which wreaked havoc on the unprepared Islands. Old records suggest that at least half of the population was wiped out before the horde was dealt with, simultaneously solving their overcrowding issues and causing irreparable damage felt to this day. This event became known as the Fog Disaster. The Abyssal hordes are claimed to have been lead by a "winged titan that screamed with joy at our demise," but actual records of the event remain unclear as to what this meant. Possibly for the first time since their original settlement, the Islands united to wipe out the Abyssal hordes in a grueling affair.

The Fog Disaster the catalyst to the first Tribunal's creation. The Tribunal was originally made to discuss the possibility of unification, which against all odds actually succeeded. The Islands united under the banner of the Galeo Alliance. The Alliance sought to expand their territory and improve their lack of resources. First experimenting with population control and agricultural developments, they found no way of sustaining their nations by relying on the islands alone. They initiated diplomatic attempts with Regalreia in an attempt to establish trade routes and cooperate on southern expansion. Regalreia never responded, inciting anger and resentment amongst the Alliance's leaders. After a long council, the Alliance agreed on a new plan: conquest. 

The resulting conflict with Regalreia became known as the Blood Moon's War, ended within a month under a moon obscured by red clouds after a disastrous invasion attempt that dissolved the Galeo Alliance overnight. The Alliance's inability to travel through the mainland without succumbing to the Curse forced them to invade by ship, which failed horribly after Regalreia's own navy ambushed them. The Alliance's airships found no success in landing safely near the city, struggling to avoid defensive ballistics in the wide open plains of the desert. The fleets remained locked in tentative combat for a few weeks before the Alliance's leaders, in their attempt to retreat, were overwhelmed by Regalreia's counterattack. The Tribunal dissolved and the survivors of the battle returned to the Islands, where conflicts began anew within a few generations.

As the scars of the Blood Moon's War faded, the Islands began a serious effort from multiple disjointed nations to investigate Galeo's Curse so that they can find a way to inhabit the unused Blood Desert's territory as their own. The Islands selected groups from each nation to represent their own, sending them to the desert to explore Ancient cities and nearby waters. Despite their best efforts, they were unable to find anything of substance over a period of a decade. The mass of symptoms recorded skyrocketed their understanding of the Curse and they became prolific in treating it. This discovery, however, did not allow them to live in the Blood Desert as they had hoped. The survivors who had gone to explore came back as neutral parties, having bonded during the journey. They founded the second and current Tribunal, aiming to maintain peace and facilitate further initiatives as the one they had just returned from.

Conflicts deescalated substantially with the arrival of the Machiya Merchant Fleet,. The Fleet drastically stimulated their trade potential and unlocked a reliable way to accrue resources through Elderwood. The Tribunal eagerly welcomed them, recognizing that they may be able to potentially secure a permanent way for the Islands to sustain themselves. While political instability remains an issue, the Galeo Islands are gradually improving their economic output and becoming more self-reliant through their wood exports.
