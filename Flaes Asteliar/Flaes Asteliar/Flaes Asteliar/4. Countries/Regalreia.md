
Regalreia, meaning "of excellence" in the language of the region's Ancients, refers to the southwest empire on the Galeo continent.

Regalreia is located on the elevated southern region of Galeo's mainland a safe distance away from Galeo's infamous bays. Despite the instability of the Galeo Islands just north of Regalreia, the empire has suffered very little turmoil since its founding at the end of the Celeste Destia. Regalreia has been ruled by the same royal bloodline since its founding.

Regalreia is not particularly sophisticated or unsophisticated. They have similar literacy rates to Arkadia. Most of their resources, if not harvested naturally, are obtained using existing Ancient architecture such as water filtration systems and air circulation turbines. Regalreia experiences mild civil unrest from discontent citizens occasionally, but has not undergone any major conflicts within the past two centuries. Regalreia is not a particularly popular destination for immigration, as its proximity to the Blood Desert and isolation from major trading hubs raises several inconveniences along with minor diplomatic concerns with other powers.

## Diplomacy

Regalreia currently has poor relations with most of the major nations in Flaes Asteliar. The current emperor, Emperor Hashar, is notorious for turning down requests for international cooperation. Namely, he has refused to take part in Arkadia's invitation for political outings as well as Ardentia's requests for cooperation in suppressing the Heart's Abyss crisis. While not outwardly hostile, his arrogant demeanor has caused substantial friction with foreign emissaries. Regalreia is on particularly bad terms with Judicia, which has had its Inquisition denied any and all entry into Regalreian territory. Regalreia's trade routes remain open for all to pass through with minor taxation.

## Trade

While trade routes remain open in Regalreia, it is not a popular hub for foreign traders due to its far distance and lack of exclusive resources other than select luxuries. Regalreia is largely self-sufficient, only relying on foreign trade to export their specialized goods and to import various amenities that are inconvenient to acquire otherwise.

Regalreia operates two main sources of exports: fruits and silk. Regalreia's fruit trade takes advantage of Galeo's tropical climate and warm winters to maintain fruit production all year long, being one of the few sources of off-season fruit that do not undergo steep price hikes. While they maintain a healthy variety of fruit types, they are most well known for their payamelons, a type of sweet large fruit that dissolves into liquid easily. Regalreia's silk trade is similarly popular due to a strong foundation of technique that the nation's workers invested in since long ago. The empire's silk is considered a luxury, though not a particularly urgent one.

## Military

Regalreia's military is built around defensive strategy more than actual war. The empire's cities are perhaps some of the most well-equipped to deal with naval assaults, possessing large fleets of water-based ships that patrol the seas at high speeds. City walls are equipped with heavy siege equipment meant to shoot down airships, and they possess an exclusive fleet of airships ready to defend their skies if necessary. Regalreia maintains a small knight order and no standing army, likely intending to procure one on the spot should the need arise. Mercenaries are an uncommon sight despite the lack of a standing army— likely due to a lack of significant threat from Abyssals or magical beasts.

## Organizations

Regalreia's major leaders and representatives are part of a council known as the Disciples, likely referencing Shafur the Sage's original disciples when Regalreia was founded. Much of the empire's bureaucratic matters are entrusted to the Disciples. While the emperor must sign off on major decisions, the level of trust and familiarity between the emperor and his Disciples results in quick approvals and transparent decisions. The only duties that the emperor personally publicly attends to are diplomatic envoys, the anointment of new Disciples, and the knighting ceremonies of new knights. Though minor disagreements arise between the emperor and the Disciples, their relationship is fairly amiable.

Regalreia leads a single knight order called the Oracle Knight Order. The Oracle Knight Order is named after the founding Sage's alleged prophetic powers, which he used to lead the original people to safety. The Oracle Knight Order answers directly the emperor. Despite their name, the Oracle Knight Order possesses no prophetic powers that have been seen publicly. They are, however, aggressive intelligence gatherers and spies who infiltrate their own cities to root out potential threats. Though it is rumored that the Oracle Knight Order has gone as far as to infiltrate other countries, there is no substantial evidence to prove it. The emperor has neither confirmed or denied the rumor.

## History

The origin of both the Islands and Regalreia traces back to Shafur the Sage, who led the survivors of the Celeste Destia in the region to safety near the bays. According to well known Regalreian legend, the Sage insisted that group move south towards where Regalreia is now founded. The majority of the Sage's people were reluctant to leave the well-developed homes of their cities in what would be called the Blood Desert. The Sage eventually left with his believers, moving south and founding Regalreia. Those who chose to stay, however, quickly discovered Galeo's Curse and migrated north to the Galeo Islands. Islander legend, on the other hand, paints the situation in a different manner. It claims that the Sage had not actually warned the people, instead opting to take a small portion of the people he could control before the Curse had fully manifested. The ones left behind, when they tried to move south, were driven out by the Sage's newly founded nation and forced to live on the northern Islands. While the details of the Sage's involvement have conflicting records, it is generally agreed that a power struggle split the original Ancient civilization and caused each fracture to go where each nation is located today. Scholars estimate that most of the settlers who originally moved north succumbed to the Curse before they had fully traversed the continent.

The Sage's initial pilgrimage was not without difficulty. Early documents extensively detail swathes of magical beasts and Abyssals that needed to be cleared away. In particular, the current capital of Regalreia required significant military force to reclaim. While the city had been long abandoned during the Celeste Destia, rogue constructs from both sides of the war kept the city an uninhabitable death trap until Shafur the Sage's initial conquest into its streets. It is estimated based on ancient graves that at least a quarter of the original pilgrimage died in the battle for the city. The Sage's survivors secured the capital city after an arduous battle, though much of the southern region remained filled with danger. The death toll incurred by the Sage's decision to move south raised major discontent, many believing that their relocation was unnecessary. Shafur, a former lord who had yet to earn his title of Sage, did not have the influence to organize the survivors to any major extent.

The legend of the Sage would only begin as survivors from those who remained north began to reach the city. The people's opinions quickly began to change as they bore witness to Galeo's Curse first-hand. Shafur, who had been undergoing growing outcries of dissatisfaction, quickly became praised as a genius Sage who could see into the future. His momentarily questionable but rewarding leadership continued as he denied entry to select portions of the newcomers, deftly avoiding a food crisis that would strike just a month later. His shrewd timing on campaigns to secure neighboring cities would catch major threats off guard, striking just as constructs would begin cycles of inactivity. The people's faith for him soon escalated into near-fanaticism. Shafur established the Regalreia empire properly, having secured most of Galeo's southern region. His most faithful followers, deemed the Disciples, would advise him on matters and act as the representatives of his will.

Shafur's last feat would come with the unification of the Galeo Islands, as he would warn his Disciples of an imminent northern invasion while on his death bed. While he would not live to see the invasion come to pass, his warning spurred Regalreia's forces into action. The Galeo Alliance's campaign was rudely halted upon their arrival as Regalreia's ships ambushed them from all directions. Anti-airship siege equipment, hastily built following Shafur's last warning, quickly foiled any attempt that the Galeo Alliance made at an invasion by air. The Blood Moon's War ended in less than a month and the Galeo Alliance dissolved after most of their leaders were killed in combat. His last message, known to Regalreians as the Sage's Last Gift, would cement him in Regalreian history as an eternal hero. Whether Shafur the Sage truly was a prophet or simply a competent leader remains to be proved, but most scholars lean towards the latter.

Regalreia's first international conflict began with the Judician Inquisition's first arrival from the north. The Inquisition requested permission to enter Regalreia's territory to investigate undisclosed matters shared with the ruling emperor in private. The emperor's scribes recorded what appeared to be an escalating altercation as the Inquisition audibly accused the emperor of an unspecified crime. As weapons were drawn on both sides, the Inquisition eventually relented and left, never to be allowed inside Regalreian borders ever again. The relationship between Judicia and Regalreia remains hostile to this day.

The rest of Regalreia's history was largely uneventful. Shafur's lineage would continue to rule the empire to varying degrees of success, though none has managed to perform so poorly that the country underwent irreparable damage. Regalreians credit this stability to the Disciples, who ensure a certain amount of decentralization and competence even when the emperor is subpar.
