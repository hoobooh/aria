Old Arkadia is a continent that was originally a part of Primera before the world's collapse following the Celeste Destia. It is the third largest continent after Galeo. Old Arkadia sits north of Primera and west of Rue and Rakir. Old Arkadia has no known population. The continent includes the capitol of Old Arkadia, formerly simply known as Arkadia, along with countless other cities that dot its landscape.

Old Arkadia is a frozen wasteland, never undergoing any temperature shifts other than to occasionally make the place colder. Judging from the terrain, it is very likely that Old Arkadia was originally filled with plains and sprawled mountain ranges. Ancient texts and maps support this, noting that the capital was founded "where the ground was greenest and the lakes shined with the stars." Now, however, the trees have been frozen solid and several feet of snow covers the ground. The weather in Old Arkadia is not particularly varied; the continent remains completely silent and serene until one of its regular blizzards sweep through the entire continent. While technically habitable at its southern coastlines, the sheer cold of the blizzards and inability for anything to grow keeps any potential settlers from even considering the place. Although no expeditions have ever successfully made it deep into Old Arkadia, it has been consistently reported that the cold only worsens to unnatural blistering the deeper in one goes. It is unclear what causes this extreme climate; many scholars argue that the weather is not of natural creation but of some great magic that remains active to this day.

Not even Abyssals frequently approach this land. Those who do usually end up frozen along with the rest of the terrain within the span of a few hours. While there are reports of distant sailors seeing giant ice beasts roaming the continent, there have been no widespread sightings. Occasional magical beasts and predatorial creatures do roam the seas nearby, but Old Arkadia is arguably the second safest place in the world for anyone who can withstand the agonizing cold.

There are only speculations as to what the capital, buried deep within Old Arkadia, is currently like. Ancient texts document that the current Old Arkadia continent froze completely within the span of hours before the world collapsed. There were no communications from within, and no survivors made it out to tell the tale. Some believe that the capital and the many cities frozen in the staggering cold have been preserved so well that they could theoretically be thawed and brought back to life, given enough time and effort. Others believe that the capital may actually be warm and well, hiding within the cold veil for one reason or another. Whatever the truth may be, the center of Old Arkadia is unreachable by ground or by air. Ironically, perhaps, the freezing weather seems to have prevented Old Arkadia from slowly crumbling the way the other continents currently are.
