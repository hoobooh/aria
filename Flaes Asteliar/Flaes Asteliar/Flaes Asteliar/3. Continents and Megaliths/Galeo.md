Galeo, derived from *Galies*, is the second largest continent in Flaes Asteliar. It sits south of Judicia and west of Arkadia, but is so far from all other continents that it is by far the most isolated major landmass in the world. It is a somewhat populated continent, housing roughly 8 to 10 million people. The continent contains thirteen different nations, each occupying a different island. While most of those islands undergo constant political upheaval and changes in both name and government, the nation that occupies the mainland has remained firm since the end of the Forgotten Era. Galeo's primary ruling nation is Regalreia, an absolute monarchy.

Galeo's climate differs drastically depending on whether it is the mainland or the Galeo Islands. The Islands are tropical in nature, with a few sporting long sandy beaches that are oddly devoid of life. The mainland, however, is dry and lacking any real vegetation. Mana crystal veins spread across the surface from the large crater that dots the surface, but creatures that live there are so prone to disease and sickness that most life chooses to stay as far away from the crater as possible. The north and center of the mainland are covered in a large white desert mixed with red crystal. While plain during the day, the sand sparkles red in the setting sun, making people call it "the Blood Desert." That reputation is further enhanced by the odd weather in the region, which snows soft flakes of red mana crystals down during the spring. In fall, the red dust rises back into the sky, marking the sky with shimmering red dots before repeating the process all over again. In Southern Galeo, however, life is much more bearable. The high elevation of the region and the far distance from the primary crater prevents the usual symptoms of sickness from occurring. Trees with long thin leaves and intricate bark twist and turn into beautiful shapes, and Regalreia flourishes despite its hostile environment.

Sickness is Galeo's biggest concern. Galeo's nations have since their founding documented what was deemed Galeo's Curse, a mysterious illness that causes latent magic inside creatures to change composition or go haywire. Galeo's Curse makes the body tear itself apart from the inside, internal functions slowly shutting down as more of the magic sustaining the host's life breaks apart. The sickness is not always fatal, as minor instances of it often go away after some time and the host makes a full recovery. The two craters dotting the coastline are undoubtedly the source, but no real explanation has been given regarding how the craters are causing such a thing. Stray Abyssals occasionally make it to the coastlines and magical beast wander close, but Galeo's Curse appears to be indiscriminate in its workings. No major threat has managed to persist in the uninhabited areas of Galeo without succumbing to the sickness.

Galeo, despite having the smallest ocean, lays claim to the strongest naval fleet in the world. Likely due to the constant conflicts between islands as well as the need to avoid the east ocean that emits Galeo's Curse, naval fleets and ships are constantly sailing through Galeo's western waters. Islands that are not directly occupied by nations are used as neutral zones by Galeo's leaders, meant for emergency repairs and diplomatic meetings. Between the craters lies an abandoned fortress city called *Sashaeisdia* (City of Sand) from the Forgotten Era, uninhabitable due to its proximity to Galeo's Curse. A few ambitious explorers who survived brief expeditions into the city tell tales of a still-lit stoves, warm food on tables, yet not a single person in sight. Others, however, tell tales of a dead city more crumbled and ruined inside than other ancient ruins— a claim that has been verified many times by passing airships.
