Reis, derived from their original founder *Renga*, are a continent split in two. It is the smallest continent in Flaes Asteliar but holds a dense population of 8 to 12 million people spread throughout its two main bodies. The continent includes Rue and Rakir, which split into different nations soon after their founding. Reis sits east of Old Arkadia and Primera but west of Judicia.

Reis has a dry airy climate tinted with snow to its west. The west side of the continent is filled with towering plateaus blanketed with soft snow and valleys of pink-leaf trees that shimmer with crystal flakes. The east side has silky white beds of sand that sprawl across into large plains dotted with ancient red-blossom trees bearing flowers. In Eastern Rue, however, much of the land is filled with hostile marshlands that bear refuge to all sorts of magical beasts and predators. The temperature shift from Old Arkadian seas into Ruien seas causes much trouble for both landmasses too; large storms are a common occurrence and cities choose to avoid the western coastlines.

Shielded by the western plateau ranges, Reis sees little threat from Abyssal hordes. Stragglers are easily picked off by the Rakiran navy. Lauded the cultural heart of the world, Rakir exercises its easy trade routes and protects them fiercely. The megalith line from Rakir to Primera in particular remains a point of danger and value for Reis, requiring frequent patrols and maintenance to ensure that traders can travel between the two continents freely. Reis is also unique in that it has successfully tamed its greatest threat— wyverns. Wyverns are native only to the Ruien continent and were once active as prolific predators, only to be tamed in recent years and turned into the two nations' protectors.

Reis was, prior to the world's collapse, already a separate continent from the rest of the world. If the continent was originally part of Primera, it drifted apart so long ago that even the Ancients knew nothing about it. Local legends conflict on whether the Remnants came and colonized the land or the Ruiens were descended from another progenitor race. Small unnaturally shaped islands also dot the Ruien seas; their shapes and resemblance leads most explorers to believe that they are the fossilized corpses of old Titans, extinct beings said to grow larger than cities and fly in the skies. While some have signs of ancient civilization, they are left alone out of reverence by the local populace.
