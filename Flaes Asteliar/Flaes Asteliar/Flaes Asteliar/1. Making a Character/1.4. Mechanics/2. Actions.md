  

Dash (Action): Existing 5e dash. Increase your AT by 17.
    
Consume Item (Bonus Action): Consume an item that you possess. Increase your AT by 20.
    
Grapple (Attack): Existing 5e grapple. Increase your AT by 20.
    
Standby (Action + Bonus Action): Decrease your AT by 10 after your turn ends. If you do not move when you use this full action, decrease your AT by an additional 15.
    
Disengage (Action): Opponents cannot perform Attacks of Opportunity on you until the end of your turn. Increase your AT by 17.
    
Prepare (Action): You can prepare an attack of your choice. The next time you would use that attack, do so with advantage. Increase your AT by 5.
    
Dodge (Action): You can impose disadvantage on all attacks against yourself until the start of your next turn. Increase your AT by 15.
    
Deflect (Reaction): You can discard your base AC for the next attack, relying only on your deflect AC to try and force deflects.
    
Ready (Action): Ready an action of your choice to use it as a reaction once until the start of your next turn. Increase your AT by 5.
    
Search (Action): Perform a basic perception check on your local area, attempting to reveal hidden details. Increase your AT by 30.
