Merchants have an innate talent for befriending people and persuading others. They are extremely effective in social situations but may have difficulty in the middle of fights.

# Class Bonuses

**Armor:** Light armor

**Saving Throws**: Intelligence, Charisma
**Skills:** Choose two skills from Arcana, Deception, History, Insight, Religion, Persuasion, and Perception
<big><big>Equipment Starter Pack</big></big>
You can start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your race and background:

- (a) fur armor and handaxe or (b) leather armor and dagger
- (a) 16 darts or (b) light crossbow and 12 bolts or (c) pistol -4
- Diplomat's pack and 425 gp in any currency of your choice

# Class Features

## People's Person

At 1st level, you are great at earning people's trust and making friends. Increase your CHA by 8.
## Appraiser

At 18th level, you have a sharp intuition for the value of different items. Gain advantage on checks to determine an item's value.
## Haggler

At 38th level, you are an excellent haggler and can effectively reduce prices on any trade. Gain advantage on social checks when haggling prices or discussing trade terms.
## Risk Reader

At 58th level, you can recognize poor business partners when you first meet them. Gain advantage on insight checks to determine a person's nature.
## Flexible Trader

At 78th level, you can adopt the social customs and quirks of whatever place you're in with ease. You can immediately adapt the mannerisms of any comprehensible society with complete naturalness.
## Stone Cold Heart

At 98th level, you can hide your true emotions like it's nothing. All insight checks against you fail.
