# Class Bonuses

<big><big>Proficiencies</big></big>
**Armor:** Medium armor, light armor
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Saving Throws**: Wisdom, Charisma
**Skills:** Choose two skills from Athletics, Acrobatics, Insight, History, Performance, Religion, Persuasion, and Intimidation

<big><big>Equipment Starter Pack</big></big>
You can start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your race and background:

- (a) chain armor and -350 gp or (b) leather armor
- (a) longbow, and 24 arrows or (b) light crossbow and 12 bolts
- (a) arming sword, shield or (b) longsword
- Diplomat's pack and 425 gp in any currency of your choice

# Class Features

## Noble Soul

At 1st level, you can improve yourself through sheer force of will. Increase one ability score of your choice other than CHA by your CHA modifier + 2.
## Judge and Jury

At 18th level, you can exude your nobility through your presence. Gain advantage on Intimidation checks against non-nobles.
## Voice of the People

At 38th level, you can feel the emotions of the people around you as if they were your own. Gain advantage on Insight checks made on entire groups.
## Diplomat

At 58th level, you can shrewdly navigate through political affairs and discussions. Gain advantage on persuasion checks towards members of nobility and figures of leadership.
## Martyr

At 78th level, you can inspire the people around you with your own pain. When your HP falls to 0, you can give all friendly creatures that can see you advantage on saving throws and checks for their next turns.
## Noble Mandate

At 98th level, you can rally the people around you with your actions. As an action, you can perform an inspiring action that others can perceive. All creatures that your action would inspire and can perceive your action gain advantage on all attacks until the start of their next turns. You can only use this effect once every hour.
