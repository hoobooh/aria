# Class Bonuses

<big><big>Proficiencies</big></big>
**Armor:** Light armor
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Wisdom

<big><big>Equipment Starter Pack</big></big>
You can start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your race and background:

- (a) padded armor and dagger or (b) leather armor
- (a) longbow, and 24 arrows or (b) light crossbow and 12 bolts
- (a) rapier or (b) bastard sword
- Burglar's pack and 775 gp in any currency of your choice

# Class Features

## Sneaky

At 1st level, you are adept at hiding your presence and moving around without being caught. Gain a +10 bonus in Stealth or Sleight of Hand.
## Appraiser

At 18th level, you have a sharp intuition for the value of different items. Gain advantage on checks to determine an item's value.
## Keen Eye

At 38th level, you are sensitive to the environment around you, letting you see hidden traps and contraptions. If you would fail a passive perception check by less than 7, you feel a strong sense of uncanniness towards your environment.
## Nocturnal

At 58th level, you can move through darkness comfortably. Nonmagical darkness cannot blind you.
## Assassin

At 78th level, you can feel what an opponent's weaknesses are. When you deal 0 or more damage to a creature, you know the exact values of any toughness attributes that were applied.
## Nimblefoot

At 98th level, you uncannily quick on your feet. Gain 15 ft. of movement and gain advantage on all DEX saves.
