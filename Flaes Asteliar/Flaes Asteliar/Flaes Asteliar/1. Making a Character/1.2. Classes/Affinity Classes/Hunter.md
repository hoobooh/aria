# Class Features

## Sneaky

At 1st level, you are adept at hiding your presence and moving around without being caught. Gain a +10 bonus in Stealth or Sleight of Hand.
## Forager

At 18th level, you are resourceful in the wilderness. Gain advantage on Nature checks and find double the food supplies you normally would.
## Tracker

At 38th level, you are particularly keen on traces of activity. Gain advantage on Investigation checks to track someone.
## Brushwalker

At 58th level, you can move through brushes and other obstacles without trouble. You can move through difficult terrain without incurring a movement penalty.
## Farsight

At 78th level, you can see far into the distance to weed out any prey that might be hiding. You can clearly see double the distance that you normally would.
## Beast Hunter

At 98th level, you instinctively know the best ways to take down large beasts. Gain a +10 bonus to your attack rolls when attacking a creature that is at least one size larger than you.
