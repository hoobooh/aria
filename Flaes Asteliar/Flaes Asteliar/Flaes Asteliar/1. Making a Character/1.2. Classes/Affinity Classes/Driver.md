# Class Bonuses

**Armor:** Light armor
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Intelligence

<big><big>Equipment Starter Pack</big></big>
You can start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your race and background:

- (a) padded armor and dagger or (b) leather armor
- (a) longbow, and 24 arrows or (b) light crossbow and 12 bolts
- (a) lance and -300 gp or (b) bastard sword
- Explorer's pack and 825 gp in any currency of your choice

# Class Features

## Pilot

At 1st level, vehicles feel as natural to you as walking. Gain a +10 bonus on any checks to pilot a vehicle.
## Belligerent Driver

At 18th level, you can fight while riding vehicles fluidly. Gain a +3 attack bonus while you are mounted or piloting a vehicle.
## Mount Tamer

At 38th level, you can handle living mounts easily. Gain advantage on checks to make living mounts cooperate with you.
## Ship Speaker

At 58th level, you can figure out the controls of any human-operable vehicle quickly. Gain proficiency with all vehicle types.
## Defensive Maneuver

At 78th level, you can manipulate your vehicle's movement to avoid enemy attacks. When you are piloting a vehicle no larger than a small airship, you can increase the AC of your vehicle by 10.
## Ace

At 98th level, you are unrivaled in vehicle handling. Automatically succeed any attempts at humanly possible vehicle maneuvers.
