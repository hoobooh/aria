Scholars have an innate talent for studying and learning. While they are better suited for non-combat activities, their versatility makes them useful anywhere.

# Class Bonuses

<big><big>Proficiencies</big></big>
**Armor:** Light armor
**Weapons:** Simple weapons
**Saving Throws**: Intelligence, Wisdom
**Skills:** Choose two skills from Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Religion, and Perception

<big><big>Equipment Starter Pack</big></big>
You can start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your race and background:

- (a) padded armor and dagger or (b) leather armor
- (a) 16 darts or (b) light crossbow and 12 bolts
- (a) quarterstaff or (b) handaxe
- Scholar's pack and 1375 gp in any currency of your choice

# Class Features

## Sharp Mind

At 1st level, your sharp mind heightens all your mental capabilities. Increase your WIS and CHA by your INT modifier.
## Cipher

At 18th level, you can deftly manipulate languages and codes. You gain advantage on checks to decipher and encrypt languages and codes.
## Bookworm

At 38th level, you can focus on tasks with razor sharp intensity and absorb knowledge quickly. When you would learn a feat or ability, you take half the time it normally would.
## Specialization

At 58th level, you can specialize into specific subjects. Gain a +6 bonus to two skills of your choice.
## Student

At 78th level, you learn new talents from the world around you. Gain 1 leveled feature from a different affinity class that is given at 18th level.
## Genius

At 98th level, you excel at everything you know. Gain expertise on all skills that you are proficient with.
