# Class Features

## Main Lead

At 1st level, you take the spotlight in any performance or act you do. Gain a +10 bonus to Performance.
## Alluring Illusion

At 18th level, your words are mixed with deceptive allure, tempting any who listen to believe you. Gain advantage on Deception checks.
## Imitator

At 38th level, you can mimic and recreate things you've seen and heard to near perfection. During a short rest, you can recreate an image or song you've heard since the start of your last rest, but that creation can still be distinguished as a fake.
## Envy

At 58th level, you can push yourself to do better when someone performs better than you. Once per short rest, when you and at least one other creature would perform the same check at the same time, you can change your dice roll to match theirs.
## Shining Star

At 78th level, you are an unparalleled performer whose shine snuffs out the light of others. When you and at least one other creature would perform Charisma based checks at the same time, you can change the result of any checks that had lower results than yours to be 0.
## Artistic Ego

At 98th level, your ego lets you choose death over failure. If you would fail a mental save, you can instead take psychic damage equal to the DC of the save you failed.
