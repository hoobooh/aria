## Channeling

At 5th level, you can imbue your weapons with power, causing them to flare up with elemental energy. As a bonus action, you can declare one elemental damage type: the weapon you are currently holding deals extra damage of that type equal to your Channeling Dice. You can end this effect as a bonus action and you can have only one such effect active at a time.
## Martial Arts

Element Tamer martial arts are unique in that they have explicit Action Delay costs; activating their effects will immediately incur the Action Delay listed on the right. Choose martial arts up to the amount of points you have. You can change your martial arts when you take an Ability Score Improvement.

## Elemental Specialist

At 30th level, you begin developing an affinity for a specific element. Choose one damage type you can channel when you obtain this feature. Reduce the Action Delay of your martial arts by 25% (rounded up) when you use a martial art imbued with that element. Gain resistance to that damage type. These effects are only active when it is possible for you to imbue that damage type.

## Elemental Expertise

At 60th level, you are now an expert in the element you chose to gain affinity with. Further decrease the Action Delay of your martial arts when using that element by 25%. As an action, you can telekinetically manipulate an amount of mass no bigger than a 10 ft. long cube comprised of that element if it is within 45 ft. of you.

## Elemental Mastery

At 90th level, you are a master of your chosen element. Become immune to your chosen element. You can sense any mass comprised of that element if it is within 180 ft. of you, letting you know its general direction as well as its size.
## **Incarnation**

At 100th level, you can become an incarnation of your chosen element. Once per short rest, you can activate the following **Exceed Art** as a bonus action:
- Your body changes composition to be made entirely of that element.
- You become resistant to physical damage.
- Increase the range and effect size of all your martial arts by 100%.
- Double your Channeling Dice.
You can only maintain your incarnation for up to a minute.
