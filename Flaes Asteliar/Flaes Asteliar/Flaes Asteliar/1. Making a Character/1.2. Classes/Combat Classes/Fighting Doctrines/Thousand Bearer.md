Master of all trades, unfamiliar to none. A Thousand Bearer holds an innate mastery over a vast armory of weapons, synergizing them in unorthodox combinations. They're masters of improvisation and thrive in chaotic battlefields.

## Weapon Savant

At 5th level, you become adept with every single weapon that you are proficient with. Additionally, you become proficient with two different exotic weapons of your choice.
## Martial Arts

## Adaptive Weapon Strike

At 30th level, you can flow between different weapons at ease. As a bonus action, if you have performed at least 2 attacks this turn, you can perform a weapon attack with a weapon that differs in one of the following characteristics from all the weapons you have attacked with this turn:
- The weight class of the weapon (light, heavy, or neither)
- The range type of the weapon (ranged or melee)
- The category of the weapon (simple, martial, or exotic)
- The physical damage type(s) of the weapon
- The number of hands required to use the weapon (versatile weapons can be treated as either)

## Weapon Transmutation

At 60th level, you can directly manipulate the forms of the weapons under your control. Once per short rest, as a bonus action, you can turn a non-magical weapon in your possession into any other non-magical weapon of the same reach category that is at most the same weight as it.

## Walking Armory

At 90th level, you bond with up to three specific weapons of your choice. Those weapons can be stored within the essence of your magic, allowing you to hide them within yourself. Drawing any of these weapons from your storage is instant. You can only store a total weight of weapons that does not exceed your maximum carrying capacity, but these weapons do not weigh anything while inside your storage.
## **Endless Armory**

At 100th level, you are no longer just the bearer of a thousand. Your will constitutes the concept of weaponry. Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you can treat every single weapon within 60 ft. of you that is not being carried by a creature as one that you possess, applying the following the effects: 
- When you touch a normal weapon that you possess, you can attempt to instantly transform that weapon into any generic simple or martial weapon of your choice. That new weapon must be the same or lower weight as its previous form. 
- You can call any weapons that you possess to your hand. The effects of **Endless Armory** last for 1 hour.
