## Ki Circulation

At 5th level, you can channel Ki energy through your body. Apply the following effects permanently:
- Gain Physical Defense *x*d2 and Elemental Defense *x*d2, where *x* is equal to the maximum amount of Ki you can store.
- When you perform an unarmed melee attack, gain a 1d10 bludgeoning damage bonus.
- You can treat your unarmed melee attacks as attacks that have the finesse property.
- You can treat your fists as a weapon that you are adept with.
- You can use your bonus action to perform 1 additional unarmed melee attack.

## Meditation

At 30th level, you can restore your Ki by performing a meditative routine. When you rest for at least thirty minutes, you fully restore your Ki to its maximum.

## Improved Ki Circulation

At 60th level, your Ki strengthens your body on a more fundamental level. Apply the following effects:
- Gain advantage on one saving throw type of your choice.
- When you perform an unarmed melee attack, gain an additional 1d10 bludgeoning damage bonus.
- When you meditate, remove all mundane negative effects.
- When you would perform a death saving throw, you can expend 1 Ki to auto-succeed that save.

## Inner Peace

At 90th level, you have achieved true inner peace through your meditation. When you successfully meditate, you can heal up to your maximum hit points and regain up to 1 Astella Surge charge. Then, you gain 3 temporary Ki points that are not counted by your maximum Ki.
## **Unified Spirit**

At 100th level, your Ki has unified your body and soul into seamless harmony. Once per short, as a bonus action, you can activate the following effect for the next minute:
- When an effect requires you to expend Ki, you can choose to not expend any instead.
- When you expend a Ki point, you heal 2d10 hit points.
- At the end of your turn, regain 1 Ki point.
- Restore all your Astella Surge charges when you activate **Unified Spirit**.
