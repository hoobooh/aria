Blitz Bladers are capable of blindingly fast attacks, overwhelming their adversaries through sheer cutting power. Strength can be overcome by speed. Insurmountable strength can be overcome by insurmountable speed.

## Blitzkrieg

At 5th level, you became faster. Reduce the Action Delay of all your melee weapons by 30% (rounded up). Increase your speed by 10 ft.
## Astella Arts

At 10th level, you gain access to unique Blitz Blader Astella arts. Choose Astella arts up to the amount of points you have. You can change your Astella arts when you take an Ability Score Improvement.

| Astella Art        | Description                                                                                                                                                    | Astella Art Points Required |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------: |
| Finesse Adept      | When you are wielding a melee weapon with the finesse property, you can perform an attack with your bonus action using that weapon.                            |              1              |
| Overwhelm          | When you start your turn and a creature is within melee range, you can immediately perform a melee attack on that creature.                                    |              1              |
| Perfect Rhythm     | This turn, if you have successfully landed at least 3 melee attacks and you have not missed a single attack, you lose 30 Action Time after your turn ends.     |              1              |
| Perfect Start      | When you perform a melee attack on your turn: if you have not moved and this is your first melee attack this turn, you can perform this attack with advantage. |              1              |
| Relentless Pursuit | As a full action, target 1 point that's no further than your speed \* 2. Immediately move there then end your turn.                                            |              1              |
| Second Wind        | Once per turn, as a bonus action, you can gain temporary hit points equal to the your Action Time / 5.                                                         |              1              |

## Feint

At 30th level, you can convert your missed attacks into feints. You can perform one more melee attack any time you miss a melee attack. You cannot apply this effect on an attack caused by this effect.
## Greater Astella Arts

At 40th level, you gain access to an additional set of Astella arts. Choose Astella arts up to the amount of points you have. You can change your Astella arts when you take an Ability Score Improvement.

| Astella Art       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Astella Art Points Required |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------: |
| Blader Kata       | If you perform a melee attack without having moved or having performed any other actions/bonus actions/reactions since your last melee attack, you can perform this attack with advantage.                                         |              2              |
| Blink Step        | Increase your range for Attacks of Opportunity by up to 10 ft. but only if you choose to move that same distance towards the target creature as part of your attack.                                                               |              2              |
| Preemptive Strike | When you would be attacked by someone with melee range, you can immediately perform one responsive attack on them.                                                                                                                 |              2              |
| Slash Draw        | You can use a bonus action to sheathe your melee weapon, and if you do, apply the following effect when you unsheathe that melee weapon as an action: immediately use that weapon to perform a melee attack with double advantage. |              2              |

## Unstoppable Strike

At 60th level, you can carry yourself forward with the momentum of a perfect cut. When you deal critical damage using a melee weapon attack, you immediately set your Action Time to 0.
## Exalted Astella Arts

At 70th level, you gain access to a final set of Astella arts. Choose Astella arts up to the amount of points you have. You can change your Astella arts when you take an Ability Score Improvement.

| Astella Art        | Description                                                                                                                                   | Astella Art Points Required |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------: |
| Dismantling Strike | Gain a permanent Slashing Bonus 7.                                                                                                            |              3              |
| Flashing Gale      | As an action, you can run to any point within 50 ft. When you pass a creature while in this movement, you can perform a melee attack on them. |              3              |
| Speed Overload     | Once per short rest, immediately set your Action Time to 0 as a reaction.                                                                     |              3              |
## Speed Debt

At 90th level, you can strain yourself to move faster than you're normally capable of. Increase the Action Time limit for reactions to 300.
## **Blade Tempest**

At 100th level, your strikes blur into a tempest of strikes. Once per short rest, you can apply this **Exceed Art** for the next minute as a bonus action: when you perform an attack, you can immediately perform another attack. You cannot use this effect on an attack caused by this effect.
