Those who laugh in the face of imminent danger. Desperados engage in high intensity ranged frontlining, constantly treading the line between risk-chasing and sheer insanity. With their blinding arrows and bullets, Desperados are frequently some of the most aggressive fighters on the battlefield.

## Thrillseeker

At 5th level, your performance skyrockets when you're in high pressure situations. Apply the following effects:
- Gain advantage on all ranged attacks on targets within 15 ft.
- Gain disadvantage on all ranged attacks on targets further than 30 ft. away.
- You cannot be flanked.
- Your ranged attacks do not trigger Attacks of Opportunity.

| Point Blank Punch | Gain a +5 bonus to your main damage type when you perform a ranged weapon attack on an adjacent target. You do not incur disadvantage from being adjacent to a hostile creature.                                                   |              1              |
| Revolving Chamber | When you make a ranged attack with a weapon that has the loading property, you can reload that weapon as part of your attack. You still incur any action delays associated with reloading that weapon.                             |              1              |

## Showdown

At 30th level, your hands are faster than feet can run from. You can perform Attacks of Opportunity using your ranged weapons on anyone that would leave a 15 ft. radius of you.
## Greater Astella Arts
At 40th level, you gain access to an additional set of Astella arts. Choose Astella arts up to the amount of points you have. You can change your Astella arts when you take an Ability Score Improvement.

| Astella Art      | Description                                                                                                                | Astella Art Points Required |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------: |
| Desperado Kata   | While you are wielding nothing but light ranged weapons, you can make two ranged weapon attacks using your bonus action.   |              2              |
| Emergency Supply | Thrice per short rest, you can magically create one piece of mundane ammo for a weapon of your choice as a bonus action.   |              2              |
| Love Tap         | Your ranged attacks automatically do critical damage to creatures that are critical.                                       |              2              |
| Zero In          | If you have hit the same target at least two times this turn, you can make one additional attack on that target this turn. |              2              |

## Gambit

At 60th level, you can kick your body into overdrive in a high-risk maneuver. When an attack is declared on you, you can activate this effect: choose a number between 1 and 40, and if that number is present within the dice output (as a digit or entire number) of that attack roll, increase your AC by that number just for that attack. If it isn't, that attack automatically does critical damage if your declared number is higher than the original attack roll and increase that attack roll by your declared number.

## Flashing Fire

At 90th level, you can shoot at overwhelming speeds. Reduce the Action Delay of all your ranged attacks by 40% (rounded up).
## **Daredevil**

At 100th level, you've crossed the boundaries of common sense, betting your all into frightening maneuvers. Once per long rest, you can activate this **Exceed Art** as a bonus action. For the next minute, apply the following effects:
- Set your current remaining hit points to 7.
- Set your maximum hit points to 7.
- Gain True Defense 7
- Gain True Bonus 7
- Gain +7 AC.
- All your ranged attacks automatically deal critical damage when they hit.
