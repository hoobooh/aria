The old faithful. Simple, straightforward, yet effective. Guardians are martials who make up for their lack of extra options with a sturdy foundation. Guardians excel at extended combat, protection, and are hard pressed to find a situation where they aren't impactful.

## Dedicated Conditioning

At 5th level, your body has undergone great conditioning to become stronger. Apply the following effects:
- Gain Physical Defense 2d8.
- Gain Elemental Defense 2d8.
- Gain a +2 attack bonus on all attacks.
- Reduce the Action Delay and loading times of all your weapons/attacks by 20% (rounded up).
- Immediately undergo an Ability Score Improvement.
- Gain advantage on one saving throw type of your choice.
- Gain 1 feat.
## Deft Strike

At 30th level, your attacks land swift and true. Increase your critical range by 3.
## Masterful Strike

At 60th level, your attacks are masterfully accurate and strike your enemies' weak points effectively. Further increase your critical range by 3. Whenever you perform a critical attack, decrease your Action Time by 10.
