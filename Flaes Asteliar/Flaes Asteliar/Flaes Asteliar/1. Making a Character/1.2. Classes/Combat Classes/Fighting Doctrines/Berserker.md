Fueled by rage, bloodlust, and instinct. Berserkers channel their raging emotions into pure power, brute forcing their way through any situation with beast-like ferocity. Berserkers excel at powering through swarms and wreaking unrelenting havoc on the frontlines of battlefields.

## Rage

At 5th level, you can amplify a powerful emotion within yourself, your will manifesting into sheer strength and brutality. As a bonus action, you can either activate or deactivate your rage then increase your Action Time by 30. While you are raging, you gain resistance to all physical damage but you have disadvantage on all non-physical checks. Your rage ends if you go unconscious, die, or do not attack for the entirety of your turn.

## Shattering Roar

At 30th level, you can shock the world around with the emotion that you were holding back. When you enter a rage, you can consume a Astella Surge charge to make everyone around you perform a STR save with a DC of 15 + your PB. Those who fail are knocked 10 ft. back and cannot take reactions until the start of their next turn.

## Improved Rage
At 60th level, your physical body has strengthened from repeated raging. You can use a bonus action to extend your rage. Permanently gain Physical Defense 5.
## Exalted Astella Arts

## Unrelenting

At 90th level, your body can unleash the full potential of your raging. Attacks cannot deal critical damage to you. You can no longer go unconscious while you are raging. Instead, you can continue to move as normal but you must still perform your death saving throws at the start of your turn if your hit points are 0 or lower.
## **Unchained**

At 100th level, your unadulterated emotion breaks you free of your fragile mortality. Once per long rest, when you die, you can immediately revive at full hit points and become immortal until the end of your next turn. Then, you can choose to increase your Action Time by up to 200. While you are immortal, you do not take damage and you auto-succeed all mental saving throws.
