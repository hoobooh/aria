Accursed. Shunned. Feared. Witch Doctors tread the line between genius and insanity, partaking in risky and volatile spells that have the potential to harm as much as help. While more aptly classified as support casters, a Witch Doctor can be just as if not more terrifying to take on in an actual fight.

## Chaos Whisper

At 5th level, your soul fluctuates drastically, making your spells no longer consume static amounts of mana. Instead, you must consume an amount of mana equal to 1d(X\*2), where X is the original total cost of that spell. If the total consumption of that spell would exceed the amount of mana you currently you have, you take the remaining cost as psychic damage. This effect does not let you attempt to cast spells that you would not be able to otherwise pay the mana cost for.
## Spellbook

At 10th level, you gain access to the Witch Doctor spell table.
## Spontaneous Theory

At 30th level, your magic strikes out into genius new discoveries at rapid pace with no regard for safety. When you cast a spell and your attack roll/damage roll contains the digit 4, gain a random effect from the list below by rolling a 1d30.

1: You lose half of your current mana.
2~3: You explode yourself in a 5ft. radius for 3d8 fire damage. Other creatures caught in that explosion must succeed a DEX save against your spell DC or take 3d8 fire damage. Then, you consume 4 mana.
4: You cast same spell again with the same parameters. This cast still consumes mana.
5~7: Creatures that were directly affected by your spell gain 20 THP. Consume 2 mana for each creature affected.
8: You immediately heal 3d15 HP.
9~10: You consume your mana by the amount spent on your spell.
11: Immediately cast a random spell from the Common Spell Table on yourself even if you do not know the spell.
12: Immediately start glowing with the brightness of a soft candle for the next minute.
13~14: Gain advantage on all checks and saves until next turn.
15: Gain disadvantage on all checks and saves until next turn.
16: Become Silenced until the start of your next turn
17: Immediately return back to life with 1 HP the next time you die within the next minute if it was from a damage source of less than 1000.
18: Manifest a clone of yourself at any available space that you have direct line of sight to within 60 ft. Any damage taken by that clone is transferred to you and it disappears after an amount of Action Time has passed equal to the cost of the spell you cast. That clone takes its turn right after yours and consumes mana directly from your pool.
19: Gain double advantage on all perception checks for the next minute. During this effect, you have disadvantage on all wisdom saving throws and take 1d8 psychic damage any time you must make a wisdom-related check or save.
20~24: Reduce your current Action Time by 40 (min. 0).
25~29: Increase your current Action Time by 40.
30: Choose and apply two different effects above in any order of your choice.

## Lingering Madness

At 60th level, your spells can no longer contain their chaotic breakdown of the world's self-perception. Creatures that have been directly hit by your spell attacks and healing must undergo a mental save of their choice against your spell DC the next time they attempt to perform an action. If they fail, consume but cancel that action.
## Fragment of Truth

At 90th level, you've grown closer to the true potential of your own Aulera. Spontaneous Theory also activates on the digits 2 and 3 in addition to the existing digit 4. When you roll for a random effect in Spontaneous Theory, you can choose to consume 1d7 mana and reroll your effect.
## **Affront to Reality**

At 100th level, you can twist the reality of the world to suit yourself. Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you can activate this effect for the next minute: Spontaneous Theory triggers on all digits. Rerolling random effects no longer costs any mana. Every time an instance of "Lingering Madness" successfully cancels an action, decrease your Action Time by 30 (min. 0), regain 1d30 mana, and heal 1d30 HP.
