A versatile all-rounder that can find a way to contribute in any situation. While being the most versatile, Mages are also capable of grand scale as they have access to the heaviest destruction spells known to the common world. Utilizing their large spell selection, Mages have no trouble finding a role in any group or party.

## Magus

At 5th level, you've become a magic savant who pursues the truer basic form of Aulera, deepening your connection with your spells. Reduce the mana cost and casting delay of two spells you know by 25% (rounded up). You can change those two spells when you take a long rest.

You require a spell focus to cast class spells. Your spell focus must be carried in your hand for you to cast your class spells. You can create a spell focus by selecting any nonmagical item and channeling magic through it for an hour. You can only have one spell focus at a time.
## Spellbook

At 10th level, you gain access to the Mage spell table.
## Mana Restoration

At 30th level, you can quickly recuperate small amounts of mana to keep yourself going. As an action, you can restore up to 15 mana then increase your Action Time by 50.
## Resplendent Aulera

At 60th level, your Aulera is a cut above the others. Increase your maximum mana by 75. Increase your maximum arcana points by 10. Increase your spell DC by 3.
## Spell Thief

At 90th level, you can forcefully generate spells normally outside of your reach. Once per long rest, you can access any spell table and cast a spell of your choice.
## **Eclipse**

At 100th level, you can amplify your spells far beyond any other. When you cast a spell from your own spell table, you can quadruple that spell's casting delay, action delay, and mana cost, then multiple all dice quantities in that spell's description by 3.
