A rare variant of casters that can sling spells at astonishing speeds in exchange for power and scale. Flash Casters are durable, highly reactive, and are capable of matching martials in toe-to-toe battles without falling behind. They excel in high-intensity situations and are self-reliant to an extreme degree.

## Flash Cast

At 5th level, you can cast spells in a flash. Reduce the casting delays of all your spells by your DEX score (min. 0). Reduce the action delay of all your spells by 35% rounded down. Choose 1 casting component (vocal or somatic); ignore it for all spells.
## Spellbook

At 10th level, you gain access to the Flash Caster spell table.
## Spellslinger

At 30th level, you can sling spells like an unrelenting rain. Reduce the action delay of all your spells by an additional 15% rounded down. When you cast a spell without a casting delay as an action, you can cast one more spell. 
## Gale Stance

At 60th level, your casting reflexes are as light as the wind. When your Action Time is below 80, you can reactively cast any spells of Action speed or faster if they do not have any casting delay.
## Unrelenting

At 90th level, you chew through low-level spells as though they're nothing. Any spells that have a total mana cost less than your Auleric level can be cast for free.
## **Unlimitation**

At 100th level, your casting blurs into a blinding flash as you hurl an entire avalanche of spells at your enemies. Once per short rest, as a bonus action, you can activate the following effect for the next minute: every time you cast a spell that has no casting delay, you can immediately cast one more spell. You cannot chain this effect onto itself.
