A nature-oriented caster that has an innate connection the natural world around them. Druids are capable of conversing with vegetation and animals, as well as commanding both to come to their aid. Druids, while not particularly preparation-dependent, shine when active in the right environments.

## Rootspeak

At 5th level, you have a deep connection to the most basic lifeforms of this world. You can converse with vegetation and fungi, exchanging basic information and able to relay basic requests.
## Spellbook

At 10th level, you gain access to the Druid spell table.
## Wildspeak

At 30th level, you have a deep connection to the advanced lifeforms of this world. You can converse with animals and bypass basic spoken language requirements, though you will be unable to engage in sophisticated dialogue. The quality of your conversation will depend on the animal you are talking to.
## Synthesis

At 60th level, you can draw the life energy from nearby plants and willing animals. As an action, you can transfer up to a total of 20 HP and 15 mana from vegetation and willing animals within 15 ft. of you, then increase your Action Time by 70.
## Eternity

At 90th level, you can bypass the curse of death by embedding yourself into the world. When you die, you can transfer your consciousness to something within 30 ft. you can converse with using Rootspeak or Wildspeak, and if you do, you revive from the vessel of that creature after a long rest has passed. If your vessel is destroyed, you can choose another vessel with the same requirements immediately.
## **Garden of Eden**

At 100th level, you can bring forth a garden of unparalleled life into the world. Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you can amplify the growth of every willing simplistic creature with 120 ft. of you to gigantic proportions of your choice (max 100x). Affected life with no room to grow cannot. Amplified creatures have 10x their normal maximum HP and 5x their normal ability scores. While you are in contact with a creature that has been amplified by this effect, you recover 20 mana and 50 HP at the start of your turn.
