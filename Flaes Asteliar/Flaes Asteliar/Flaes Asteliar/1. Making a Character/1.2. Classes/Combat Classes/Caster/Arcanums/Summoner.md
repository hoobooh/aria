Strength in numbers, and safety in even more numbers. Summoners, as their namesake implies, rely on summoned creatures. They use lengthy summoning sequences to create a wide array of creatures that can fulfill any role necessary. Summoners are versatile and can be extremely potent, but require significant preparation time and protection.
