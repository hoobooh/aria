A caster of gentler nature that heals rather than harms. Sages specialize in direct healing and protection, possessing little in terms of self-reliant combat capability. Sages thrive in tightly knit groups where they can enhance the performance of their nearby allies.

## Healing Touch

At 5th level, your soothing touch permeates your healing. When your spell would heal a creature(s), heal them and yourself (apply only once if target is yourself) by an additional 1d4 for every Auleric level you possess.
## Spellbook

At 10th level, you gain access to the Sage spell table.
## Aura of Protection

At 30th level, your protective aura maintains a vigilant guard of everyone around you. Allies within 15 ft. of you gain advantage to all saving throws while you are alive.
## Protective Embrace

At 60th level, your spells wrap around your targets with an additional layer of protection. When your targeted spell successfully reaches its target, you can give that target 25 THP.
## Damage Ward

At 90th level, your protective magic has fused with yourself, making you significantly more durable. Gain resistance to 3 damage types of your choice, as well as True Defense 1.
## **Paradise**

At 100th level, you manifest a utopic paradise unto yourself. Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you can activate this effect for the next minute: all allies within 240 ft. are protected by your Aura of Protection. When an ally under your Aura of Protection starts their turn, you can immediately target and heal them 1d20 HP, and when you do, that heal is treated as a spell.
