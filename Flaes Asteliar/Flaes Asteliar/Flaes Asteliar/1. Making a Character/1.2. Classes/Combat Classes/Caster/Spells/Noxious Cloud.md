
Range: 60 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): 10

Action Delay (AT): 10

Mana Cost: 6

Arcana Point Cost: 3

Auleric Level: 2

Description:

You permeate a foggy 5 ft. radius cloud of poison at a location you see in range. Creatures that enter that cloud for the first time that turn, start their turn inside the cloud, or are within the cloud when you first cast this spell must succeed a CON save or take 1d10 poison damage. That cloud lasts until the end of your next turn.
Deal an additional 1d7 poison damage for every Auleric level you possess above 1.
