
Range: Touch

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 55

Mana Cost: -

Arcana Point Cost: 1

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You create a ball of light of any color of your choice that illuminates everything around the spot you touched in a 30 ft. radius. If the target you touched moves, the ball of light moves with it.
