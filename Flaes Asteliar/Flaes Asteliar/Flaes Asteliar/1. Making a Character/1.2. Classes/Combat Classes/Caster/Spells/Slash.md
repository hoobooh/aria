
Range: 60 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 16

Mana Cost: 3

Arcana Point Cost: 1

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You perform a cutting attack at a nearby target you see. Make a melee spell attack against that target. On a hit, deal 2d11 slashing damage.
Deal an additional 1d11 slashing damage for every Auleric level you possess above 1.
