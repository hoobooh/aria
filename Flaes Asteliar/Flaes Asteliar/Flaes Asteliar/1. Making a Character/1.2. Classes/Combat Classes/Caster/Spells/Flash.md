
Range: 15 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 30

Mana Cost: 6

Arcana Point Cost: 3

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You emit a blinding crack of light around you that inflicts the Blinded condition for 1 turn on anyone within 15 ft. that fails a WIS save against your spell DC, provided that they are affected by your bright lights and noises.
