
Range: -

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 50

Mana Cost: 5

Arcana Point Cost: 2

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You unleash a strong bolt of lightning at a target you see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against that target. On a hit, deal 4d6 lightning damage. Then, you can attempt to pull that target 5 ft. towards you.
Deal an additional 2d6 lightning damage for every Auleric level you possess above 1.
