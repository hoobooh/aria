
Range: Touch

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 55

Mana Cost: 3

Arcana Point Cost: 3

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You touch a weapon you are wielding. That weapon deals an additional 2d8 damage of any elemental damage type of your choice. This effect lasts until you hit something with that weapon, lose touch contact with that weapon, or 10 turns pass. When you cast this spell, you can multiply the mana cost by an amount up to your Auleric level, and if you do, add an additional 1d8 damage for every 2 levels you multiplied by. This effect cannot stack.
