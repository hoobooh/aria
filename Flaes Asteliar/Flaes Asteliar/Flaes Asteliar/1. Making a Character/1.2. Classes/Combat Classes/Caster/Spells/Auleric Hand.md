
Range: 30 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 55

Mana Cost: 1

Arcana Point Cost: 

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You manifest a spectral hand with an amount of HP equal to your maximum arcana points, an AC equal to your Auleric level, and 10 in every ability. You can move that hand remotely, but you cannot cast spells using it. This effect lasts for 1 minute, after which you can repeatedly choose to consume 1 mana to increase the duration by 1 minute. You can only have 1 Auleric Hand active.
