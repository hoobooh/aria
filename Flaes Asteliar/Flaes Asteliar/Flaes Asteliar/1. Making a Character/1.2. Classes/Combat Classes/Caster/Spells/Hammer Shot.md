
Range: 80 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 30

Mana Cost: 2

Arcana Point Cost: 1

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You shoot a simplistic bolt of magic at a target you see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against that target. On a hit, deal 1d13 bludgeoning damage.
Deal an additional 1d7 bludgeoning damage for every Auleric level you possess above 1.
