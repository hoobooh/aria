
Range: 60 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 45

Mana Cost: 3

Arcana Point Cost: 2

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You hurl a bolt of fire at a target you see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against that target. On a hit, deal 2d8 fire damage then ignite the target if it is flammable.
Deal an additional 1d8 fire damage for every Auleric level you possess above 1.
