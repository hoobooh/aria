
Range: -

Speed: Bonus Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 24

Mana Cost: 1

Arcana Point Cost: 2

Auleric Level: 2

Description:

You momentarily widen your senses, detecting any active spells, martial arts, and magical beasts within a 60 ft. radius of you. You know the direction and magnitude of everything detected through this spell.
