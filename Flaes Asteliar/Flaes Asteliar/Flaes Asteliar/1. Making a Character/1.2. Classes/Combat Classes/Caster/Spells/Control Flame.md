
Range: 30 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 40

Mana Cost: 2

Arcana Point Cost: 1

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You control a ball of flame you see no larger than a 1 ft. radius within range. You can move that ball up to 60 ft., but it cannot be more than 30 ft. away from you. This effect lasts for 1 minute, after which you can repeatedly choose to consume 1 mana to increase the duration by 1 minute.
