
Range: 50 ft.

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): -

Action Delay (AT): 43

Mana Cost: 4

Arcana Point Cost: 2

Auleric Level: 1

Description:

You bring forth a freezing gale of air at a target you see within range. Make a ranged spell attack against that target. On a hit, deal 2d10 cold damage then expel any weak flames at the target.
Deal an additional 1d10 cold damage for every Auleric level you possess above 1.
