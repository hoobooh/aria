
Range: -

Speed: Action

Casting Delay (AT): 80

Action Delay (AT): 26

Mana Cost: 17

Arcana Point Cost: 6

Auleric Level: 4

Description:

You expansively widen your senses, detecting any active spells, martial arts, magical beasts, enchantments, artificer creations, as well as any spells that were recently cast within 240 ft. of you.
You know the direction and magnitude of everything detected through this spell.
