Pepirians are a race of small spotted half-humanoids from the Forgotten Era. Pepirians are skittish and energetic, supplementing their short statures with optimism and cunning. While technically omnivorous, they generally display a strong preference for fruits and vegetables. Physically, they appear to have a thin layer of soft white fur over their body lined with dark stripes. They have short limbs and small padded hands along with large circular ears. They have round faces with large eyes that are usually spotted on at least one side with a black mark, along with a small sphere-shaped tail that seems to serve no purpose. They have hypersensitive smelling. They usually grow to be around 3 feet in maturity. They live for roughly 60 to 80 years and display little physical strength but astonishing agility.

Pepirians have been around for at least as long as the Ancients, though it is documented that they spent most of their existence in isolated forests and jungles living simple lives. With the collapse of the world at the end of the Celeste Destia, severe deforestation and habitat loss forced Pepirians to relocate closer to other civilizations until they eventually began to fully integrate into society. While Pepirians are described as friendly people with little inclination for violence, ancient texts suggest that they were an integral and vicious armed force during the Celeste Destia. Many Pepirians form small local communities in cities or the wilderness— not out of alienation, but simply because they prefer the social energy that comes with groups of their own. Pepirians have a medium population comparable to Ruiens. There are no Pepirian-dominant governments.

As a Pepirian, gain the following effects:

- You gain -7 to STR, +7 to DEX, +3 to CHA, and -3 to CON.
- You gain Physical Defense -2d7, Elemental Defense 1d9.
- You have a speed of 45 ft.
- You know Arkadian and Pepirian.
- You gain 1 feat of your choice.
