The Bright Peoples of Flaes Asteliar, also commonly coined the Bright Peoples or the Peoples, are the sentient races in Flaes Asteliar that possess sentience and self-awareness. The usage of the word "Bright" as a descriptor refers to the potential for included races to become Astellas, who harness the magic of the world freely. Despite sharing a single name, the races bear no form of unified solidarity in their shared existence. Racial tensions remain a strong source of conflict in the world, being particularly present within altercations between the Chimeras and Centurials.

The Bright Peoples of Flaes Asteliar were originally referred to as the *Sienlaes* by the Ancients. Rough translations of the term equate to "starving and of sight," likely referring to the sentient and self-aware races' capability to reject basic instincts and desires such as hunger. *Laes*, translating roughly into "sight," was a way to categorize the races that possessed similar eyes— that being most of the humanoid races. It's unlikely that most of the races today were included or even existed during this period; physical similarities and historical overlaps indicate that some were at some point derived from Remnants.
