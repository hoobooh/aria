Enkas are a race of small feathered people from the Forgotten Era. Enkas tend to be eccentric and inquisitive people that have a habit of obsessing over things, often being called one track minds. Enkas, despite their timid appearance, prefer meat over vegetables and eat larger portions than other races. Physically, Enkas appear to have smooth skin heavily obscured by gray-tinted feathers and hexagonal indents in their skin near joints. They possess long feathers on their back and forearms akin to tail feathers, but they do not possess the ability to fly. They possess sharp carnivorous teeth and large round eyes. They have hypersensitive hearing, but their eyesight leans towards the worse end. They grow to roughly 3 feet in maturity. They live for roughly 130 to 150 years and are strangely resistant to heat.

Enkas appear to have originated from near the Forius continent, where their stout gray appearances blended in with the rocky mountains and traversed the heat-infused hazards without issue. They maintained a technologically advanced but isolated civilization in Forius, which was known as Fortus back then. It is believed that Enka engineers were responsible for the creation of the first Titans, which were mechanical behemoths made during the Celeste Destia. Enkas, even after the end of the Forgotten Era, remain fairly isolated from the world. While many of their medium population remain in Forius where they are natively built for, many also integrate into common society without issue. The only Enka-dominant government is the Machiya Merchant Fleet, which roams the skies all around the world.

As an Enka, gain the following effects:

- You gain +1 to STR, +1 to CON, and +3 to INT.
- You gain Fire Defense 1d8.
- You have resistance to fire damage.
- You have a speed of 30 ft.
- You know Arkadian and Fortissian.
- You gain 1 feat of your choice.
- You can make an unarmed attack with your mouth that deals 1d26 piercing damage and has an action delay of 36.
