Centurials are mechanically fabricated runed humanoids made of metal and biological components. They are commonly known to be unstable and erratic, many having been damaged by the wear of time and other dangers. Centurials, despite their machine-like appearance, possess nerves and the same five senses that others do. Centurials need to eat biological food in order to maintain the biological parts of their body. Physically, they possess smooth skin interrupted by metal plates and exposed machinery. Their machine-like areas vary drastically, but their biological areas resemble that of humans or Remnants. They are usually around 4 to 6 feet. Centurials do not seem to age naturally, their physical ages often dating well into the Forgotten Era. Once active, however, they cease functioning quickly in around 40 to 60 years. They display extreme durability but suffer poorly from an inability to properly heal.

Centurials are the most recognizable product of the Ancient war effort during the Celeste Destia. The methods with which they were created are not entirely lost, though the sophistication required to recreate such a process does not currently exist and most major governments strictly forbid attempting such a thing. Centurials were originally humans and Remnants that underwent extreme transplants to mechanically enhance their capabilities grown in Ancient facilities from a comatose state in birth. Most legendary of the Centurials were the Old Arkadian *Destesaer*— literally translated to Wings of Death or more commonly known as the Deathwings, a legion of Centurial knights equipped with runed winged armor that fought from the skies. Centurials are a dying race; their population only grows when mass-produced caches of undisturbed Centurial hibernation chambers are discovered. While some Centurials can reproduce, their children possess no mechanical traits and are merely humans or Remnants. By far the rarest race of the Peoples, Centurials that do not form small communities with their discovered caches end up scattered across the world with no particular preference. There are no Centurial-dominant governments.

As a Centurial, gain the following effects:

- You gain +10 to CON, -2 to CHA, and -2 to DEX.
- You gain Physical Defense 1d8, Slashing Defense 2d8, Piercing Damage 1d2, Fire Defense 1d8, and Lightning Defense -1d8.
- You have resistance to healing and you gain Healing Penalty 1d8.
- You only gain a quarter of your maximum hit points back during a short rest.
- You have resistance to necrotic damage.
- You have a speed of 30 ft.
- You know Arkadian and 1 of Ruien or Rakiran.
- You gain 1 feat of your choice.
