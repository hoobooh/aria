Ruiens are a proud race of humanoids descended from wyverns. Originating from the high altitude mountain tops of Reis, their hypersensitivity to magic causes many Ruiens to avoid magic dense areas such as the Abysses and underground crystal deposits. Ruiens seem to prefer fruits and vegetables over meat due to their hypersensitivity towards life-based magic. Physically, they appear to have smooth skin with patches of rough, almost hide-like areas near their ears, chest, arms, hips, and feet. Their ears, similar to Remnants, are sharp and point downwards but are significantly longer and have two sharp points rather than one. Their teeth are noticeably sharper than that of other species. It is said that ancient members of Ruien royalty bore large wings on their backs, but there is no reputable evidence for this. They usually grow to be around 6 to 7 feet in maturity. They live for roughly 80 to 100 years and display frail constitutions compensated with agile movement.

Ruiens are, according to local legend, descended from the first Ruien who was made by an ancient wyvern that evolved into a magical beast. The first known recorded instance of a Ruien was some time during the Celeste Destia, where other historians argue that they were made as part of the same cause that birthed the first Chimeras and Centurials. This theory remains disputed due to the large distance from Reis and Primera, but it's generally agreed that the Celeste Destia was the catalyst for their creation. Ruiens are a fairly populous species but reside primarily in Reis, which is populated almost entirely by Ruiens and Chimeras. The only Ruien-dominant governments are the Rakiran Dynasty and the Ruien Dynasty, which have been locked in a tense power struggle since the creation of their nations.

As a Ruien, gain the following effects:

- You gain 5 to DEX and -5 to CON.
- You gain Mystical Defense 3d8 and Physical Defense -2d6.
- You have resistance to force damage.
- You have a speed of 40 ft.
- You know Arkadian and 1 of Ruien or Rakiran.
- You gain 1 feat of your choice.
