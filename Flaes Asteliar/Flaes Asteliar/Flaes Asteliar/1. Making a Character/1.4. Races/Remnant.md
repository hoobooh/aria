Remnants are the direct descendants of the Ancients. They are known to be somewhat prideful, though that trait is likely derived from their common place in Astelian high society rather than inherited. Remnants are observed to have smaller appetites than their counterparts and bear a strong connection to Abyssals, who seem to prioritize attacking them over other races. Physically, they appear to have smooth skin with sharp ears that point downwards and sharp molars that chew through meat easily. Their hair grows quickly and flows with a strange silk-like ripple to it, usually bearing light colors with few exceptions. They usually grow to be around 4 to 5 feet in maturity. They live for roughly 100~120 years and display minor resistances to magic.

Remnants are one of the progenitor races of the Ancients. Their appearances match the ones found in old texts and paintings, and their current place in global society indicates so too. Remnants live in small quantities inside the Primera continent, their population remaining on a downward trend due to their tendency to mingle with other races— namely humans, who possess similar traits and easily erase the genetic traits of Remnant physiology. Remnants bear a unique responsibility in Ardentia where they comprise the Abyss Guard, an elite knight order dedicated to containing the Abyssal threat pouring out of the Heart's Abyss. The Remnant-dominant governments in Flaes Asteliar are the Ardentian Council, Regalreia's monarchy, and some of the leaders in the Galeo Islands.

As a Remnant, gain the following effects:

- You gain +2 to INT, +1 to DEX, and +2 to CHA.
- You gain Elemental Defense 2 and Psychic Defense 1d6.
- You have a speed of 30 ft. 
- You know Arkadian, Ardentian, and Ancient.
- You gain 1 feat of your choice.
