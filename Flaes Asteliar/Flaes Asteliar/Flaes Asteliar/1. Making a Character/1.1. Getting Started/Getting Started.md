So you've decided to make a character. Here's the general ordered list of what you should get done. First things first, give [[On the World of Flaes Asteliar]] a quick read so you know what kind of world you're getting into. Don't worry, it's not long. You should give the rest of the world's details a quick read too, but you can save that for until you know what you're supposed to.

Once you have a general idea of Flaes Asteliar as a world, head on over to the list below. If you're already familiar with building tabletop characters, you can probably ignore the list's ordering quite a bit. Go wild.

#### 1. Make a copy of the Aria5e GSheet.
Access the sheet [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1KMCNWmLN7Y58jpPhdx_rMOWBSTMlziYsDp9QL49vLUE/edit?usp=sharing).
Make a copy and name it whatever you want. Don't worry about filling it out yet; you'll flesh out the details bit by bit.
You might notice that this is an extended version of Derixyleth's v2.1 GSheet for Avrae; note that the original sheet and DiceCloud will *not* work for Aria5e.

#### 2. Choose a class and race.
And a name, if you want. Some people prefer to save that for last. Go through the list on the left and find something that appeals to you, then fill in your GSheet accordingly.

#### 3. Choose a background.
And by background, that doesn't just mean the mechanical background (though you should choose that too). Make your character an actual character, not just a mechanical shell. Consider, of course, the bond/flaw system already inside your GSheet. Consider how your character would actually interact in the world; a character that is prone to conflict and uninterested in the plights of your campaign probably won't have a great time there. 

Here are some basic questions to get you going; use them or don't.

Where was your character born? Why did they leave?
How does your character feel about religion? Do they follow any particular faith?
What is your character's dream(s)? What keeps them moving in this perilous world?
Does your character have any family? Friends? Colleagues?
Is your character afraid of anything? Anything they just can't stand?
How moral is your character? A radiant hero, a dashing rogue, or perhaps an average day-to-day person capable of both good and evil?

#### 4. Assemble your gear.
You're almost done! Your class, race, and background should have specified what gear you can bring. By default, Aria5e starts you off with 2000 gold pieces along with whatever is provided by your character selections, but you should discuss this with your DM to make sure that's the amount they intend for you to start off with.

If you'd prefer not to dig through item lists, that's fine! Aria5e's classes has suggested starting packages that simplify the process for you. Keep note of your carry weight too. Your speed and DEX will go down with how overweight you get. Be careful! Make sure you record all your gear, equipped or not, in the Inventory tab of your GSheet.

#### 5. Choose a name (If You Haven't Already).
The title's pretty clear. Choose a name! Try to be mindful of your character's past: different cultures have different naming styles, after all.

#### 6. Play.
You're done! Import your GSheet into Aria5e and go wild.
