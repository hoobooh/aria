Chimeras are a very diverse group of magical beast-implanted humanoids made during the Celeste Destia to help stifle a losing war. Their temperaments vary drastically depending on the beast traits they inherited, ranging from hyper aggressive to extreme timidity. It is difficult to assign any traits to them due to the large number of Chimera subraces, but they are generally prone to prioritizing their instincts over rational thought and thus have difficulty getting along with other races. Physically, their skin tends to bear strong resemblance to their inherited traits around the arms, spine, and head. They usually grow to be around 4 to 7 feet in maturity. They live for roughly 60 to 80 years and display dramatic physical superiority and recovery.

Chimeras are almost certainly the product of the Ancient war effort during the Celeste Destia. While the methods involved were either lost or purposely hidden, there are plentiful ancient texts documenting the advent of the Chimeras, born using the corpses of various magical beasts. It's generally believed that the Chimeras lost most of their warlike traits after generations of intermingling, resulting in a unique collection of subraces that no longer present the same danger they did before. Chimeras live in small quantities primarily in Rakir, Arkadia, and Fortis. There are no Chimera-dominant governments other than some leaders in the Galeo Islands.

As a Chimera, gain the following effects:

- You gain +3 to STR and +3 to DEX.
- You gain Physical Defense 2, Elemental Defense 2, Necrotic Defense 1d3, and Force Defense 1d2.
- You have resistance to necrotic damage.
- You have a speed of 30 ft.
- You know Arkadian and 1 of Ruien or Rakiran.
- You gain 1 feat of your choice.
