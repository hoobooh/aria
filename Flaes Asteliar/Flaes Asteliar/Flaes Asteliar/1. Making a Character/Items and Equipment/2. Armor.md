Those anticipating combat will quickly find themselves in need of armor to shield them from whatever dangers they may face. While sturdier pieces of armor might help reduce more damage, it's important for these combatants to consider whether the bulkiness of their armor is worth it.

Gear AC adds to the base character AC of 20.

"gp" refers to the universal value of an item. Prices will vary per location and actual purchases are usually done through existing currencies or bartering.

### Proficiency

- **Armor Proficiency:** Anyone can put on a suit of armor or strap a shield to an arm. Only those proficient in the armor’s use know how to wear it effectively, however. You can gain certain armor proficiencies from your class, race, or some other source. If you wear armor that you lack proficiency with, you have disadvantage on any ability check, saving throw, or attack roll that involves Strength or Dexterity, and you can’t cast spells.
- **Strength Requirement:** If the Armor table shows a number in the Strength column for an armor type, the armor reduces the wearer’s speed by 5 feet for every point missing unless the wearer has a Strength score equal to or higher than the listed score.

### Toughness

Most armor pieces have something called Toughness attributes, which dictate how much damage they actually prevent. Generally, heavier types of armor tend to deflect more damage, though certain types will be better than others at dealing with *specific* damage types.
