Those anticipating combat will quickly find themselves in need of weapons to fight against whatever dangers they may face. All sorts of weapons exist in Flaes Asteliar— it's important for any combatant to consider weapons that they're comfortable using to heighten their fighting prowess. The quality of their weapon and how good they are at using it are frequently the difference between life and death.

"gp" refers to the universal value of an item. Prices will vary per location and actual purchases are usually done through existing currencies or bartering.

# Weapon Properties
___________

| Property      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ammunition    | You can use a weapon that has the ammunition property to make a ranged attack only if you have ammunition to fire from the weapon. Each time you attack with the weapon, you expend one piece of ammunition. Drawing the ammunition from a quiver, case, or other container is part of the attack. Loading a one-handed weapon requires a free hand. At the end of the battle, you can recover half your expended ammunition by taking a minute to search the battlefield |
| Blockbreacher | This weapon can partially breach a target's ability to deflect. You can reduce your target's deflect AC by 5 for the duration of your melee attack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Double Form   | This weapon has two different forms. Its alternate form is specified in the parenthesis after the double form property. You can treat this weapon as its alternate form freely, mirroring its damage and properties.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Finesse       | When making an attack with a finesse weapon, you use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Heavy         | A heavy weapon is bulky and hits harder than normal. Creatures that are Small or Tiny have disadvantage on attack rolls with heavy weapons. When you make an attack with a heavy weapon, you can apply your Strength modifier twice to the your damage roll.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Light         | A light weapon is small and easy to handle, making it ideal for use when fighting with two weapons.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Loading       | The time required to reload the ammunition in this weapon is too long and requires an additional action to reload. The AT cost of reloading a weapon is specified in parenthesis after the loading property.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Range         | A weapon that can be used to make a ranged attack has a range shown in parentheses after the ammunition or thrown property. The range lists two numbers. The first is the weapon’s normal range in feet, and the second indicates the weapon’s maximum range. When attacking a target beyond normal range, you have disadvantage on the attack roll. You can’t attack a target beyond the weapon’s long range.                                                            |
| Reach         | This weapon adds to your reach when you attack with it. This property also determines your reach for opportunity attacks with a reach weapon. The reach this weapon adds is shown in parentheses after the reach property of this weapon.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Thrown        | If a weapon has the thrown property, you can throw the weapon to make a ranged attack. If the weapon is a melee weapon, you use the same ability modifier for that attack roll and damage roll that you would use for a melee attack with the weapon. For example, if you throw a handaxe, you use your Strength, but if you throw a dagger, you can use either your Strength or your Dexterity, since the dagger has the finesse property.                               |
| Two-handed    | This weapon requires two hands to use. This property is relevant only when you attack with the weapon, not when you simply hold it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Versatile     | This weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property—the damage when the weapon is used with two hands to make a melee attack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Pulling       | This weapon can attempt to pull a target 5 ft. towards you upon landing a successful hit. An athletics check may be required if the target is too heavy or resisting.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Punching      | Your weapon is treated as an unarmed attack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Heavy Draw    | You cannot attack with this bow unless you possess the same or more Strength than the DC specified in parenthesis after the heavy draw property.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Unwieldy      | Your weapon is too unwieldy to be used freely. You cannot attack targets that are within 5 ft. of you using this weapon.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
